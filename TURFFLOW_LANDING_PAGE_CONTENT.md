## 7. DOUBLE-BOOKING PROTECTION

### Main Heading:
Built to protect your slots.

### How It Works:
TurfFlow uses centralized booking logic and temporary slot holds to reduce booking conflicts across channels.

```
[ Available Slot ] ──> [ Temporary Hold ] ──> [ Online Payment ] ──> [ Confirmed Booking ]
```

* When a customer initiates checkout on any channel, the selected slot is temporarily held.
* If payment is not completed within the allowed payment window:
  ```
  [ Temporary Hold ] ──> [ Expired Window ] ──> [ Available Again ]
  ```
  The hold expires automatically, and the slot returns to the open schedule for other players.

### Coordinated Across All Touchpoints:
Website bookings, WhatsApp bookings, and owner-created bookings all run through the exact same central system. While no digital platform guarantees zero conflicts under all circumstances, TurfFlow's coordinated hold logic significantly reduces double bookings and schedule confusion.

## 18. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

### What is TurfFlow?
TurfFlow is a turf booking and automation platform that brings slot availability, WhatsApp inquiries, online website reservations, online payments, customer records, and day-to-day operations into one centralized system.

### What is the difference between the two packages?
*TurfFlow WhatsApp* (₹999/mo) provides automated WhatsApp booking, slot locking, payments, and an owner dashboard without a customer-facing website.  
*TurfFlow Complete* (₹2,499/mo) includes everything in the WhatsApp package plus a dedicated customer booking website, web booking functionality, and synchronized website-WhatsApp availability.

### How does the 2 months free offer work? Are there any prepayments?
Both *TurfFlow WhatsApp* and *TurfFlow Complete* are completely free for your first 2 months (60 days) with ₹0 software fees. There are zero prepayments, advance setup fees, or card lock-ins required to start. You can cancel anytime without penalty or commitment.

### Do I need a website?
A customer-facing website is included only in TurfFlow Complete. TurfFlow WhatsApp operates completely through WhatsApp and the owner dashboard, meaning you do not need a website to get started.

### Can customers book through WhatsApp?
Yes. Customers can interact with the TurfFlow booking agent directly on WhatsApp to check availability, choose slots, pay, and receive confirmations.

### Can customers book through the website?
Yes, with TurfFlow Complete. Players can visit your turf's booking website, pick dates and slots, and pay online.

### Do website and WhatsApp share availability?
Yes. Both channels are connected to the same centralized booking system. When a slot is booked on one channel, it is immediately updated across all channels.

### How does TurfFlow handle temporary slot holds?
When a customer selects a slot to pay, the system applies a temporary hold. If payment is completed within the allowed window, the booking is confirmed. If payment is not completed before the window expires, the hold is released and the slot becomes available again for other players.

### Can I manually create bookings?
Yes. Turf owners and managers can create manual bookings directly from the owner dashboard for walk-in players, phone reservations, or regular teams.

### Can I change slot prices?
Yes. You have full control from the dashboard to configure slot prices, peak hours, weekend pricing tiers, and custom rates.

### Does TurfFlow charge booking commission?
No. TurfFlow software does not charge a per-booking commission (0% commission). You retain your booking revenue. Third-party payment gateway charges are separate.

### Are payment gateway fees included?
No. Razorpay transaction charges are separate from TurfFlow software pricing and are handled directly by the payment gateway.

### Is maintenance included?
Yes. Managed maintenance and technical support are included with both packages, covering setup, system updates, bug fixes, and operational support. Major new custom features are handled separately.

### Can I request custom features?
Yes. If your sports facility has specialized operational requirements, custom features and additional integrations can be discussed and scoped separately.

4. Smart Slot Locking & Double Booking Prevention

To prevent schedule conflicts and duplicate reservations:

  • When a player selects an open slot, TurfFlow places a temporary hold (typically 5 to 10 minutes) on that slot across all channels.
  • If checkout is completed within the hold window, the slot transitions immediately to Confirmed state.
  • If payment is abandoned or fails, the slot is automatically released back to Available state for other players without requiring manual manager intervention.
  • Facility managers retain override capability from the Owner Console to manually lock or release slots for maintenance or offline league matches.

The Customer Journey

How TurfFlow Works

A simple, automated 5-step booking flow designed for fast conversions with zero manual friction.

01

Customer opens WhatsApp or the turf booking website

Players choose their preferred way to book—either chatting directly with the WhatsApp agent or browsing your dedicated booking website.

02

Customer checks available slots

TurfFlow instantly displays up-to-the-minute available slots and current pricing for the chosen date.

03
Helps prevent double bookings

Customer selects a preferred slot

The chosen time slot is temporarily held while the customer completes payment, stopping conflicts.

04

Customer completes the online payment

Direct, secure checkout through integrated Razorpay supporting UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking.

05

Booking is confirmed and reflected in the owner's dashboard

The customer receives instant confirmation details, and the booking immediately appears in your owner dashboard with all slot calendars updated.

1. Overview & Scope

Welcome to TurfFlow ("TurfFlow", "we", "our", or "us"). TurfFlow operates a specialized software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform facilitating turf and sports facility booking automation, WhatsApp conversational agents, online payments, customer management, and facility operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how TurfFlow collects, processes, and protects information when facility owners ("Facility Owners", "Merchants") utilize our management dashboard, and when end-user players and customers ("Players", "Customers") discover, reserve, or pay for slots through TurfFlow-powered web booking portals or WhatsApp conversational channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data to confirm reservations, prevent double bookings, process payments, and support sports venue operations:

  • Facility Information: Name of turf/facility, sports court specifications, pricing rules, operating schedules, facility address, bank account details for settlement payouts, and business contact details.
  • Customer & Booking Details: Customer name, 10-digit mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact identifier, selected sport type, booking date and time slot, booking reference ID, and transaction amount.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction reference IDs, payment status (successful, pending, failed), payment method type (UPI, Card, NetBanking), and Razorpay transaction IDs. We never see or store raw card CVVs or UPI PINs.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device type, browser information, session cookies, and interaction logs with the TurfFlow application for security and audit purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data for the following legitimate purposes:

Reservation Fulfillment Locking selected slots, sending instant booking receipts, and preventing double bookings across channels.
Operational Notifications Automated WhatsApp reminders prior to game time, schedule change alerts, and payment receipts.
Owner Dashboard Intelligence Providing facility owners with real-time revenue analytics, slot utilization rates, and player booking records.
Security & Fraud Prevention Detecting bot abuse, preventing ghost slot reservations, and ensuring reliable payment reconciliations.

4. WhatsApp & Conversational Communications

TurfFlow integrates with the official Meta WhatsApp Business API to provide conversational booking and customer assistance. When you message a TurfFlow-powered facility number or request a demo via WhatsApp:

  • Communications are governed under Meta's end-to-end encryption standards while in transit.
  • We only send transactional messages (slot confirmations, payment links, hold expirations) triggered directly by your interactions.
  • You can opt out of automated WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP or contacting our support team.

5. Payment Security & Processing

All online transactions on TurfFlow are processed securely through our RBI-compliant payment gateway partner, Razorpay.

Payment Security Standard

Payment information is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption and processed according to PCI-DSS Level 1 specifications. TurfFlow never has access to, nor stores, sensitive card numbers, CVVs, passwords, or UPI security PINs.

6. Data Retention & Storage

We retain booking transaction records, customer contact profiles, and facility logs for as long as necessary to fulfill contractual obligations, provide dispute reconciliation, and comply with applicable tax and accounting laws in India (typically up to 7 years for financial records). Non-transactional session data is automatically purged on a rolling 90-day basis.

7. Service Providers & Subprocessors

We engage vetted third-party service providers solely to execute platform operations:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: High-availability cloud servers located in secure Indian data center regions.
  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay Software Private Limited for checkout tokenization and UPI processing.
  • Messaging Infrastructure: Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for conversational messaging.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and applicable regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data held about you by TurfFlow.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your profile where retention is not mandated by statutory accounting or legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
